Civil Engineering Learning Tools

Interactive lessons for the core subjects of a civil and structural engineering degree. Each one has a live model to play with, every formula worked through with your own numbers, and exercises that check each step.

Pick a topic and press Open. Nothing to install. Topics marked planned are on the way. A topic marked needs revision works, but it is not in its final version yet.

Your progress

Two figures per topic. Answered counts the questions you got right. Points say how well, at most 3 per question. Both stay in this browser. Resetting is the only way to earn points again.

These are learning tools. They are not verified for design and do not replace a standard, a validated code or an engineer’s judgement.
